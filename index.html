Researcher at QuSoft & CWI

Galina Pass

I am a researcher at QuSoft & CWI. I study quantum algorithms and complexity, with a focus on space-efficient quantum algorithms, time-space tradeoffs, and quantum walks. Previously I was a PhD student at the University of Amsterdam (KdVI & QuSoft) under the supervision of Stacey Jeffery and Michael Walter.
